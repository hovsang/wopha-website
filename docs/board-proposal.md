# Proposal: New WOPHA Website & Online Payments

**To:** WOPHA Board of Directors
**From:** Sang Ho, [street address], WOPHA member
**Date:** July 2026

## The problem

Our current website (wopha.com, built on Weebly) is hard to use on phones,
looks abandoned (newest event photos are from 2018), and doesn't let anyone
pay dues, book a pool party, or find current pool hours without digging
through emails and Google Sheets. Weebly itself is a discontinued platform.

Two issues need attention regardless of this proposal:

1. **The resident directory is publicly accessible.** The Directory page links
   to a published Google Sheet — anyone on the internet can open resident
   names, addresses, and contact info. It should be unpublished immediately.
2. **Announcement emails expose resident addresses.** Recent announcements
   were sent with 200+ member emails in open CC rather than BCC.

## What I've built

A complete replacement site, ready to launch at wopha.com:

- **Works on phones.** Every page, tested at phone widths.
- **Online dues payment** — bank transfer (about $5 in processing cost on a
  $535 payment) or card, plus Zelle and check instructions. Payments arrive
  in the WOPHA bank account through the association's own Stripe account;
  no money ever passes through me.
- **Self-service for the common asks:** pool party requests, court
  reservations (ReserveMyCourt, including pickleball), swim team
  registration (SwimTopia), a suggestion box, and a contact-update form
  that feeds the dues email list.
- **Current-season info on real pages:** dues, pool dates and hours, rules,
  party fees, ALTA teams, board roster, minutes, covenants and bylaws.
- **A community page** built from what actually happens here: the events
  calendar, pine straw sale, graduation banner, clean-up day, ice cream
  social, Summer Fun Series, Harvest for the Hungry, parent group chats,
  and an **opt-in** family directory that is never public.
- **Anti-fraud guidance** on the dues page (after the 2026 scam email that
  impersonated WOPHA).

Ongoing platform cost to the HOA: **about $10–12/year** (domain renewal).
Hosting is free (Cloudflare Pages). The current Weebly subscription can be
cancelled after launch.

## What it would cost

| | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|
| Launch fee (one-time) | **$1,200** | $0 |
| Monthly webmaster retainer | **$75/mo** | $140/mo, 12-month minimum |

**The launch fee covers:** content migration from the old site, Stripe
payment setup with the treasurer, forms and calendar wiring, DNS cutover
with zero downtime, directory lockdown, and board email routing
(board@wopha.com, treasurer@wopha.com).

**The retainer covers:** all content updates (hours, dates, minutes, events)
within 2 business days, seasonal page turnover each spring, payment-link
changes when dues change, hosting/domain management, and help with the
announcement list (BCC hygiene or a proper newsletter tool).

**For comparison:** HOA software platforms that provide a portal like this
charge $199/month (PayHOA, at our community size — $2,388/year) to
$30–90/month (HOA Express, TownSq), every year, forever. Option A costs
$2,100 in year one and $900/year after.

## Terms

- The Association owns its content, domain, accounts (Stripe, email, forms),
  and data. I retain ownership of the site template and tooling, which the
  Association may use under a perpetual license — including after we stop
  working together.
- Either side can end the retainer with 30 days' notice. If it ends, I hand
  over everything needed for someone else to maintain the site (it's plain
  HTML — any web-comfortable volunteer can).
- **Disclosure:** I'm a WOPHA member proposing paid work to my own HOA. I ask
  that the board vote on this and record the decision in the minutes, and I
  will abstain from any member vote touching it.

## Ask

Approve Option A (or B) at the next board meeting. I can have the site live
at wopha.com within two weeks of approval.
