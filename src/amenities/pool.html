Before you go

Rules & guest policy

The short version: members in good standing swim, guests sign in, and kids under 9 bring a parent.

Who can swim
  • Members in good standing (Full or Annual) and their guests.
  • Residents who are Social members may not come as guests of other members.
  • Residents who are delinquent in dues may not come as guests of other members.
Bringing guests
  • Up to 5 guests per member household per day. Immediate family of members doesn't count against the 5.
  • All guests must sign in.
  • Local non-member guests can visit once per week (Monday–Sunday), no matter which member hosts them.
  • Out-of-town guests: unlimited visits.
  • Babysitters, caregivers, and long-term house guests are exempt from guest limits: just give the Board written notice.
Kids at the pool
  • Children under 9 must have parental supervision at the pool.
Wristbands & gate access
  • Members wear the current season's wristband at the pool. New wristbands are waiting for you at the pool the first time you come (no separate trip needed).
  • The pool and tennis gates use an access code shared with members by email. Please don't pass the code outside the membership: it's how we keep the facilities for members and their guests.
Occasional early closures
  • The pool hosts Parkview Poolcats home swim meets and the team banquet, so it closes early a handful of evenings each summer.
  • Closures are announced ahead of time on the neighborhood email list and the calendar on the Community page.

Book a pool party

Reserve the pool and pavilion for a birthday, graduation, or get-together. Two steps and you're booked.

1. Submit the request form

Fill out the Pool Party Request Form with your date, time, and guest count.

Pool Party Request Form

Create the Pool Party Request Form as a Google Form (date, time, guest count, contact info) and paste its URL into the button above.

2. Confirm with the coordinator

Joel Tarpley, our pool party coordinator, will follow up to confirm the details and collect the fee.

Timing and holiday restrictions. Parties of 25 or more guests are limited to Monday–Friday, or weekends after 6 p.m. Parties run 3 hours maximum and must end by 9 p.m. Later than 9 p.m. (until midnight at the latest) only with the President's approval. Private parties are not allowed on major holidays: Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Father's Day, the 4th of July, and Labor Day.
