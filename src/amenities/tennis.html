Two LED-lighted courts · 7 a.m. – 11 p.m. daily

Tennis & pickleball

Book a court online for tennis or pickleball, play under the lights until 11 p.m., or join one of our ALTA teams. Members age 8 and up can reserve their own court time.

Get on the court

Reserve a court

Book your slot right here on the neighborhood site. ReserveMyCourt also keeps working side by side while we switch over.

Check availability & book Reserve on ReserveMyCourt

How reservations work

  • Slots are 1.5 hours each.
  • Book up to 48 hours in advance.
  • Members age 8 and up can reserve a court.
  • Limit: 1 slot per day, 3 slots per week.
  • Non-members may not reserve courts.
  • Playing pickleball? The pickleball courts are listed separately: pick a pickleball court (for example, "Pickleball 2A") when you book.
Keep the courts nice

Court rules

Hours

Courts are open 7:00 a.m. – 11:00 p.m. daily. Both courts are LED-lighted for evening play.

Shoes & attire

Proper tennis shoes and attire are required. Shirts must be worn, and sports bras must be covered.

Kids on the court

Children under 7 need adult supervision.

Keep outside the fence

No glass, food, bicycles, or chairs inside the court enclosure.

Play with us

Not a member?

Non-members can play tennis for a seasonal fee: $45.00 per season for adults and $30.00 per season for juniors. Note that non-members can't reserve courts. For full court privileges and everything else the neighborhood offers, join as a member.
