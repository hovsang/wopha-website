How billing works

  • Subscriptions are paid to the webmaster's business Stripe account. It is entirely separate from the association's own Stripe account that collects member dues.
  • Stripe hosts checkout, emails receipts, and provides the customer portal for changing or cancelling the subscription.
  • Either side can end the arrangement with 30 days notice, per the proposal terms, with full handover.
